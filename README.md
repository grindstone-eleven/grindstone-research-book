# the companion

*a public research notebook — grindstone-eleven*

Somewhere in northern Italy, on ordinary consumer hardware, a small
system is being **raised rather than programmed**.

It runs entirely at home. No cloud, no telemetry, no data leaving the
house. That constraint is not a limitation of the project — it *is*
the project.

## What is being attempted

Most assistants are built. This one is **taught**.

- Its behaviors are not written in code: they live as **engrams** —
  discrete memories with their own triggers, grafted one by one into a
  hippocampus, recalled by resemblance, reinforced when they serve well
  and weakened when they fail.
- It has **organs** (modules with declared functions, written mandates
  and hard guardrails), **senses** (an eye that reads photographs), a
  **heart** that beats and worries, and nights in which what happened
  during the day is consolidated into proposals for the morning.
- When it makes a mistake, it is not patched. It is **corrected** — the
  error is charged to the memories that caused it, the lesson is
  grafted back, and a bench of trap-situations measures whether the
  learning actually took hold. Every score is written down. Some days
  the graph goes up.

It keeps a private calendar from dictated speech, recurring
appointments included. It files documents where it was granted
permission, and only there — writes happen at a human's touch, never on
its own. It reads a photographed shift sheet, gets it wrong, is
scolded with the specific errors, and reads it better in a fresh
conversation with no notes to lean on: the improvement lives in its
memory, not in the chat.

A training protocol has emerged from this work — four organs to align
for every new capability, a cycle of teach → run → score → reinforce →
re-measure, and a growing list of rules learned the hard way (an
engram that never wakes is a neuron that sleeps; too much procedure
and the apprentice drowns in it).

## What comes next

A model of its own, **grown from zero** on the same desk — tiny,
modern in architecture, expert on narrow things and honest about
everything else. It has a name already. It will earn its page here
when it earns its scores.

## What this repository is

The journal of the research: findings, protocols, measurements —
published as they mature.

The code, the library, the memories and the life inside them belong to
the person the companion serves. They stay in the private garden.
